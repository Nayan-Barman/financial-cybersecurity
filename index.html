Your security matters

Small security habits can make a significant difference when managing your finances online.

01 — THE BASICS

What is financial cybersecurity?

Financial cybersecurity means protecting your banking accounts, payment information, passwords and personal data from digital threats.

01

Protect your money

Good security practices can reduce the risk of unauthorized transactions and financial fraud.

02

Protect your information

Keep passwords, account information and payment details private and secure.

03

Protect your identity

Secure personal information helps reduce the risk of identity theft and impersonation.

02 — KNOW THE RISKS

Common financial threats

Understanding common scams makes it easier to recognize suspicious activity.

!

Phishing

Fake emails, messages and websites may attempt to trick you into revealing sensitive information.

×

Malware

Malicious software can attempt to steal information from your computer or mobile device.

?

Identity theft

Stolen personal information can be used to impersonate you or access financial services.

$

Payment fraud

Fraudulent payment requests and unauthorized transactions can result in financial loss.

03 — STAY PROTECTED

10 simple security habits

Make these practices part of your everyday financial routine.

01

Use strong passwords

Use long, unique passwords for your financial accounts.

02

Enable two-factor authentication

Add another layer of protection beyond your password.

03

Watch for phishing

Be cautious with unexpected links, emails and messages.

04

Never share OTPs

Keep passwords, PINs, CVVs and authentication codes private.

05

Update your devices

Install security updates for your devices and applications.

06

Monitor transactions

Regularly check your accounts for unfamiliar activity.

07

Be careful with public Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive financial activity on untrusted networks.

08

Download trusted apps

Install financial applications only from trusted sources.

09

Verify payments

Check the recipient and amount before confirming a payment.

10

Act quickly

Report suspicious activity to your financial institution promptly.

04 — QUICK CHECK

Are you protecting your accounts?

Use this quick checklist to review your security habits.

  • ✓ I use unique passwords.
  • ✓ I have two-factor authentication enabled.
  • ✓ I check my financial transactions regularly.
  • ✓ I don't share OTPs or passwords.
  • ✓ I keep my devices updated.
STAY ALERT

Think before you click.

If something feels suspicious, stop and verify it through an official channel before taking action.
